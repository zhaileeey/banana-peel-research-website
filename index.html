01 / Context

About the Study

The proposal explores whether banana-peel extract can be developed into a beverage that is acceptable to the selected sensory evaluators.

Background of the study

The rationale presents banana peel as a by-product with potential value for food and beverage development. The related literature discusses its fiber, pectin, phenolic compounds, flavonoids, minerals, and other bioactive components, while noting that processing and concentration may influence color, aroma, bitterness, astringency, and texture.

These are background points drawn from the proposal's literature review, not laboratory findings from this study.

Research objectives / questions

  • Determine mean sensory acceptability scores for color, aroma, taste, and overall acceptability.
  • Determine whether mean scores differ significantly among formulations at the 0.05 significance level.
  • Identify which formulation obtains the highest overall-acceptability mean score.

Significance of the study

Students & researchers

May provide learning material and baseline information for related food-development and sensory-evaluation research.

Consumers & producers

May encourage exploration of banana peel as an ingredient for value-added products.

Environment & entrepreneurs

The proposal frames peel reuse as a possible waste-reduction approach and a starting point for future product exploration.

Scope and delimitations

The proposal focuses on selected Grade 12 students of Anao National High School and sensory characteristics only. It excludes laboratory testing of nutritional composition, antioxidant properties, microbial safety, shelf life, and commercial viability. Its findings, once available, are intended to apply to the selected participants rather than all consumers.

The rationale presents banana peel as an underused by-product with potential value for food development and possible waste reduction. The study itself does not test nutritional composition.

Where and when will the study take place?

The proposal states Anao Senior High School Campus, San Francisco East, Anao, Tarlac, during the second term of school year 2026–2027.
